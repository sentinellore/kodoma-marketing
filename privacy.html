Privacy Policy

How we handle your data.

Effective date: May 14, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 14, 2026

Hi — Kodoma here. 👋 You're trusting us with some of the most personal numbers you have: how much you slept, what you ate, how your heart's doing. So we want to be straight with you about what we collect, what we don't, and why.

The short version: we only collect what we need to make the app work for you and your crew. We never sell your data. We don't run ads. There's no third-party tracking quietly piggybacking in the background. The longer version is below, written as plainly as we could manage while still being a real privacy policy.

Questions, corrections, or want your data deleted? Email us anytime at support@kodoma.app.

1. Who we are

Kodoma is a social health app — track your fitness, meals, and grocery runs, and share progress with the people you care about. This policy explains how Kodoma ("we," "us," "our") collects and uses information when you use our mobile app or visit kodoma.app.

2. Information we collect

Account info

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Password (stored only as a one-way hash — we never see your actual password)
  • First and last name

Profile info

The things you tell us during onboarding or edit later:

  • Date of birth
  • Sex
  • Height and weight
  • Dietary preferences (allergies, diet style)
  • Daily targets (steps, calories, sleep, protein)
  • Profile photo (optional)

Health data

With your permission, Kodoma reads a small set of health metrics from your phone's native health platform:

  • iOS: Apple HealthKit
  • Android: Google Health Connect

The metrics we read:

  • Steps
  • Sleep duration
  • Active calories burned
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Blood oxygen saturation
We store these as daily summaries — not raw samples. So we keep "you took 8,432 steps on Tuesday," not the individual minute-by-minute readings your watch recorded. Daily summaries live in our database; raw data stays on your device.

You can revoke health access at any time — in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices, in Android's Health Connect app, or inside Kodoma at Profile → Privacy & Permissions.

Camera & photos

When you choose to, you can use your camera inside Kodoma to:

  • Snap a meal photo (for AI nutrition analysis)
  • Scan a grocery receipt (for OCR parsing)
  • Take a workout selfie (for your workout recap card)

Photos you capture in the app are uploaded to Kodoma's storage so you can see them on your timeline and share them with your crew if you choose. We don't access your camera roll except when you explicitly ask Kodoma to save a workout recap photo back to your photos.

Location

If you start a run, Kodoma uses your phone's GPS to map your route and calculate distance and pace. This happens only while a run is active and the app is in the foreground. We never track your location in the background, and we never collect location data outside of an active run.

Activity in Kodoma

The things you do inside the app:

  • Workouts (gym sessions, runs, notes)
  • Meals you log
  • Grocery runs and items
  • Goals you set
  • Crew membership and crew messages

Push notification tokens

To send reminders, streak alerts, and crew notifications, we register a push token with Apple Push Notification service (APNs) on iOS and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) on Android. These tokens identify your device, not you personally.

Device & diagnostic info

Basic device info (OS version, app version) and crash logs, so we can fix bugs. No advertising IDs, no fingerprinting, no behavioral analytics.

3. AI features and third-party processors

A few features in Kodoma rely on AI services. When you use these features, the relevant input is sent to a processor and a result is sent back. Here's exactly who gets what:

  • Google Gemini Vision API — when you snap a meal photo, the image is sent to Gemini to identify the food and estimate nutrition.
  • Google Cloud Vision OCR — when you scan a receipt, the image is sent to Cloud Vision to extract the line items and prices.
  • Anthropic Claude API — when you generate a meal plan or workout plan, your dietary preferences, daily targets, and recent activity are sent to Claude to generate the plan.
All three services are used on their data-protected (zero-retention or enterprise) tiers: your inputs are not used to train their models, and they are not retained by the provider beyond the immediate request.

4. How we store and secure your data

Kodoma's backend runs on Supabase (PostgreSQL) hosted in the United States. Specifically:

  • Encryption at rest — your data is encrypted on Supabase's servers.
  • TLS in transit — all traffic between the app and our backend is HTTPS.
  • Row-Level Security — Postgres policies enforce that only you (and people you've explicitly shared with) can read your records.
  • Hashed passwords — passwords are stored using one-way hashing; nobody at Kodoma can see them.
  • HTTPS only — the app and website refuse insecure connections.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't run ads in Kodoma.
  • We don't sell your personal data. Ever. To anyone.
  • We don't share your data with advertising networks or data brokers.
  • We don't use trackers or pixels for ad targeting.
  • We don't let third parties train their AI models on your inputs.

6. What your crew can see

Kodoma is a social app, so some of your info is visible to people in groups ("crews") you join. Here's the complete list of what other crew members can see:

  • Your username
  • Your first name
  • Your profile avatar (if you set one)
  • Workouts, meals, and grocery runs you've explicitly shared with that crew
  • Your scores on shared leaderboards and challenges
  • Messages you send in crew chat

Your full name, email, date of birth, weight, height, raw health metrics, and any activity you didn't choose to share are not visible to anyone else in Kodoma. Sharing a workout or meal with a crew is always an opt-in choice when you log it.

7. Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate information (you can edit most of it directly in Profile → Edit profile).
  • Delete your account and all associated data. Open Profile → Privacy & Permissions → Delete my account. This runs a server-side delete_user() function that permanently removes your profile, meals, workouts, goals, group memberships, health summaries, and photos.
  • Export your data. Email support@kodoma.app and we'll send you a JSON export within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent for any permission (Camera, Location, Health, Notifications) by toggling it off in your device's OS settings, or in Profile → Privacy & Permissions.
  • Opt out of health data sync entirely by disabling "Use Apple Health data" / "Use Health Connect data" in Profile → Privacy & Permissions.

For GDPR-specific requests (data portability, the right to object, lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority), email us — we'll respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Kodoma is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. Depending on where you live, additional rules apply for users between 13 and 18 — for example, the EU's GDPR-K requires parental consent for users under 16 in some member states. If you're a parent or guardian and you believe your child has signed up without your consent, email us at support@kodoma.app and we'll delete the account.

9. International users (GDPR & CCPA)

Kodoma's servers are located in the United States. If you use the app from outside the U.S., your data will be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

If you're in the EU/UK (GDPR): our legal basis for processing your data is your consent (when you grant permissions) and the performance of our contract with you (when you create an account and use the app). You have the right to access, correct, delete, port, restrict, or object to processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you're in California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" — though as noted, we don't sell or share personal information in the way those terms are defined. You also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

10. How long we keep your data

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our primary database immediately and from backups within 30 days. Anonymous, aggregated metrics (e.g., total active users) may be retained longer for product analytics.

11. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top, and for any material changes we'll notify you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. The version at kodoma.app/privacy is always current.

Privacy questions, data requests, or just want to say hi: support@kodoma.app.
